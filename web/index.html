Built for real money life

Banks & transactions

Add all your bank accounts and log credits and debits. Balances update live, so you always know what you can spend.

Debts & interest

Who you owe and who owes you, with or without interest. Record part-payments and see exactly what is left.

Monthly bills

Recharges, power, WiFi, credit cards, loans, cheeti and more. Each has its date, amount and manual or auto-debit.

Reminders

Every bill becomes a reminder, before the day and on it. Auto-debit bills remind you to keep money in the bank.

Voice assistant

Powered by Gemini Live. Say what you spent, paid or lent and it is saved. Ask what you owe and get an answer.

PIN lock
